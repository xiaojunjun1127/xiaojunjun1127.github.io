什么是浮游植物藻华？

浮游植物藻华是水体中的浮游植物在适宜环境下快速繁殖， 导致细胞数量或生物量显著增加的现象。

主要浮游植物类群

  • 硅藻：具有硅质细胞壁，是重要的初级生产者。
  • 甲藻：部分种类能够形成赤潮或产生毒素。
  • 蓝藻：常在淡水及部分海洋环境中形成水华。

藻华形成条件

  • 充足的氮、磷等营养盐
  • 适宜的温度和光照
  • 相对稳定的水体层结
  • 合适的水流和风力条件

生态影响

浮游植物是水生食物网的重要基础，也参与海洋碳循环。 但部分异常藻华可能导致水体缺氧、遮挡光线或产生毒素。

10.8 九个核心问题

Nine Core Questions · Physical Oceanography

一、风和冷却分别能形成多深的混合层？

English question:
How deep is a typical mixed layer if mixed by wind? How deep can it reach if driven by cooling?

中文回答：
由风引起的湍流混合通常形成约10—100米深的混合层， 具体深度取决于风速、风持续时间和原有水体层结。 强烈的冬季表层冷却会使海水密度增加并发生对流， 混合层可加深到数百米。在拉布拉多海、格陵兰海和威德尔海等 深对流区，极端情况下可超过1000米。

English answer:
Wind-driven turbulence typically produces a mixed layer about 10–100 m deep, depending on wind strength, duration, and the pre-existing stratification. Strong winter surface cooling increases seawater density and triggers convection, allowing the mixed layer to deepen to several hundred metres. In deep-convection regions such as the Labrador, Greenland, and Weddell Seas, it can exceed 1,000 m.

二、北太平洋副热带与副极地海区的温盐垂直结构有何特点？

English question:
What are the typical vertical temperature and salinity profiles in the subtropical and subpolar regions of the North Pacific?

中文回答：
北太平洋副热带海区表层温暖、盐度较高，表层以下存在明显的永久温跃层， 温度随深度迅速降低；盐度一般也从高盐表层向下减小，并在中层出现低盐水。 副极地海区表层寒冷且较淡，温跃层较弱，冬季甚至接近等温； 盐度通常随深度增加，因为表层受到降水、融冰和较淡水体的影响。

English answer:
In the subtropical North Pacific, the surface is warm and relatively saline, with a strong permanent thermocline beneath it. Temperature decreases rapidly with depth, while salinity generally decreases from the salty surface and may reach an intermediate-depth minimum. In the subpolar North Pacific, the surface is cold and relatively fresh, the thermocline is weaker, and salinity commonly increases with depth.

三、海洋中哪些区域以净蒸发为主？

English question:
What are the dominant regions of net evaporation in the ocean?

中文回答：
净蒸发主要出现在南北纬约20°—30°的副热带海区。 这些区域受副热带高压控制，空气干燥、降水少、太阳辐射较强， 因而蒸发量大于降水量。北大西洋副热带、阿拉伯海，以及地中海和红海等 半封闭海区的净蒸发尤其显著。

English answer:
Net evaporation is dominant mainly in the subtropical oceans around 20°–30° latitude in both hemispheres. These regions are controlled by dry subtropical high-pressure systems, receive little rainfall, and experience strong solar heating. Particularly strong net evaporation occurs in the subtropical North Atlantic, the Arabian Sea, the Mediterranean Sea, and the Red Sea.

四、混合层热收支包括哪些主要部分？

English question:
What are the key components of the mixed-layer heat budget?

中文回答：
混合层热收支主要包括海表净热通量、水平平流、垂向夹卷与混合、 穿透混合层底部的短波辐射，以及混合层厚度变化带来的影响。 海表净热通量又包括短波辐射、长波辐射、潜热通量和感热通量。 海流还可将暖水或冷水输送到研究区域，改变混合层温度。

English answer:
The mixed-layer heat budget includes the net surface heat flux, horizontal heat advection, vertical entrainment and turbulent mixing, penetrating shortwave radiation, and changes in mixed-layer depth. The net surface heat flux consists of shortwave radiation, longwave radiation, latent heat flux, and sensible heat flux.

五、北大西洋平均比太平洋更咸还是更淡？为什么？

English question:
Is the North Atlantic Ocean saltier or fresher on average than the Pacific? Why?

中文回答：
北大西洋平均比北太平洋更咸。主要原因是大西洋的蒸发相对于降水更强， 而大气环流会把从大西洋蒸发的部分水汽输送到太平洋流域。 地中海高盐水的外流也会提高北大西洋盐度。 相比之下，北太平洋降水较多，而且没有与地中海高盐外流相当的盐源。

English answer:
The North Atlantic is saltier on average than the North Pacific. Evaporation is stronger relative to precipitation over the Atlantic, and atmospheric circulation transports some Atlantic water vapour toward the Pacific basin. Salty Mediterranean outflow also raises Atlantic salinity, whereas the North Pacific receives more precipitation and lacks an equivalent high-salinity source.

六、哪些海区季节差异最大，哪些区域季节变化最小？

English question:
What regions of the ocean are characterized by large differences from summer to winter? What regions have the least seasonal variability?

中文回答：
中高纬度海区的季节差异最大，尤其是北半球大陆边缘、 湾流延伸区和黑潮延伸区。冬季强烈冷却和风混合使混合层加深， 夏季加热则形成浅而温暖的表层。赤道和热带海区接收的太阳辐射 全年变化较小，因此海表温度和混合层的季节变化通常最弱。 深海也因远离海气直接作用而几乎没有明显季节变化。

English answer:
The largest summer-to-winter differences occur at middle and high latitudes, especially near Northern Hemisphere continental margins and in the Gulf Stream and Kuroshio Extension regions. Tropical and equatorial oceans generally show the weakest seasonal variability because solar forcing changes less throughout the year. The deep ocean also has very little seasonal variation.

七、维持温跃层的两个主要过程是什么？

English question:
Please describe the two processes that maintain the thermocline.

中文回答：
第一个过程是海表加热与风驱动环流。太阳加热维持温暖表层， 风应力造成的辐合、下沉和副热带俯冲把暖水输送到温跃层内部。 第二个过程是垂向混合与上升流，它把深层冷水缓慢带向上方， 并与上层暖水交换热量。暖水下沉与冷水上升、混合之间的平衡， 共同维持大尺度永久温跃层。

English answer:
The first process is surface heating combined with wind-driven circulation: convergence, downwelling, and subtropical subduction transfer warm surface water into the thermocline. The second is vertical mixing and upwelling, which slowly bring cold deep water upward and exchange heat across density surfaces. Their balance maintains the large-scale permanent thermocline.

八、哪些因素分别决定 Qs、Qb、Qe 和 Qh？

English question:
What factors determine the heat-budget terms Qs, Qb, Qe, and Qh, respectively? Make clear the importance of each factor.

中文回答：
Qs（短波辐射）主要由太阳高度、昼长、纬度、 季节、云量和海面反照率决定，其中太阳入射角和云量最重要。

Qb（净长波辐射）取决于海表温度、空气温度、 水汽和云量。海表越暖，向上的长波辐射越强；水汽和云会增加 向下的大气长波辐射，减少海洋的净热损失。

Qe（潜热通量）由风速、海表饱和比湿与近海面空气 比湿之差决定。风越强、空气越干、海表越暖，蒸发和潜热损失越大。

Qh（感热通量）主要由风速以及海表与空气的温差决定。 当海水明显暖于空气且风速较大时，海洋通过感热向大气释放更多热量。

English answer:
Qs, shortwave radiation, depends mainly on solar elevation, day length, latitude, season, cloud cover, and surface albedo; incoming solar angle and clouds are especially important.

Qb, net longwave radiation, depends on sea-surface temperature, air temperature, humidity, and cloud cover. A warmer surface emits more radiation, while clouds and water vapour increase downward atmospheric radiation.

Qe, latent heat flux, is controlled mainly by wind speed and the humidity difference between the saturated sea surface and the overlying air.

Qh, sensible heat flux, is controlled mainly by wind speed and the air–sea temperature difference.

九、四种热通量的典型范围和极值区域是什么？

English question:
What are the ranges of the magnitude of the four terms? For each term, specify the approximate location and value of its maximum and minimum center.

中文回答：
下列数值是多年平均量级，不同季节、数据集和正负号约定会造成差异。

Qs：约0—300 W/m²。最大值通常位于少云的热带和 副热带海区，约250—300 W/m²；最小值位于冬季极区，可接近0 W/m²。

Qb：海洋向上的净长波热损失通常约20—120 W/m²。 较大值常见于温暖、干燥且少云的副热带海区，约80—120 W/m²； 较小值常见于寒冷极区或湿润多云区域，约20—40 W/m²。

Qe：潜热损失通常约0—250 W/m²，冬季湾流和黑潮 延伸区在冷、干空气爆发时可超过300 W/m²；最小值出现在弱风、 高湿的赤道辐合带或部分极区，可接近0 W/m²。

Qh：感热通量通常约−20—100 W/m²， 冬季湾流、黑潮及边缘海冷空气爆发区可达到100—200 W/m²； 暖空气覆盖较冷海面时可能为负，约−10至−20 W/m²。

English answer:
These are approximate climatological ranges; exact values vary with season, dataset, and sign convention.

Qs: about 0–300 W m⁻². Maxima of roughly 250–300 W m⁻² occur in relatively cloud-free tropical and subtropical oceans, while winter polar values can approach zero.

Qb: net upward longwave loss is commonly about 20–120 W m⁻². Larger values occur over warm, dry, cloud-free subtropical waters, while minima occur in cold polar or humid, cloudy regions.

Qe: latent heat loss is commonly about 0–250 W m⁻² and may exceed 300 W m⁻² over the Gulf Stream and Kuroshio Extension during winter cold-air outbreaks. It approaches zero in weak-wind, humid regions.

Qh: sensible heat flux is commonly about −20 to 100 W m⁻² and can reach 100–200 W m⁻² during strong winter cold-air outbreaks. It may become slightly negative when warm air flows over a colder ocean surface.
